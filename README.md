# EAS 100 Midterm II: Ultimate Study Guide & Practice Exam

## Part 1: "Put in Order" Cheat Sheet (Smallest to Largest / Earliest to Latest)

1. **Hydrologic Cycle Water Reservoirs (by Volume)**
   Atmosphere $\rightarrow$ Surface water on land $\rightarrow$ Groundwater $\rightarrow$ Ice sheets/glaciers $\rightarrow$ Oceans
2. **Atmospheric Layers (by Altitude from Surface)**
   Troposphere $\rightarrow$ Stratosphere $\rightarrow$ Mesosphere $\rightarrow$ Thermosphere
3. **Composition of Earth's Atmosphere (by Gas Abundance)**
   Methane ($\text{CH}_4$) $\rightarrow$ Ozone ($\text{O}_3$) $\rightarrow$ Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$) $\rightarrow$ Argon ($\text{Ar}$) $\rightarrow$ Oxygen ($\text{O}_2$) $\rightarrow$ Nitrogen ($\text{N}_2$)
4. **Dissolved Ions in Seawater (by Abundance)**
   Bicarbonate $\rightarrow$ Potassium $\rightarrow$ Calcium $\rightarrow$ Magnesium $\rightarrow$ Sulfate $\rightarrow$ Sodium $\rightarrow$ Chloride
5. **Stages of Sea Ice Formation**
   Frazil ice $\rightarrow$ Nilas / Pancake ice $\rightarrow$ Pack ice $\rightarrow$ Solid sea ice
6. **Surface Albedo Values (Lowest Reflectance to Highest)**
   Asphalt $\rightarrow$ Open ocean $\rightarrow$ Forest $\rightarrow$ Bare soil $\rightarrow$ Grass $\rightarrow$ Sand $\rightarrow$ Bare glacier/sea ice $\rightarrow$ Older snow $\rightarrow$ Fresh snow 
7. **Carbon Cycle Reservoirs (by Mass of Carbon)**
   Biosphere $\rightarrow$ Atmosphere $\rightarrow$ Hydrosphere $\rightarrow$ Geosphere
8. **Linnaean Taxonomic Hierarchy**
   Species $\rightarrow$ Genus $\rightarrow$ Family $\rightarrow$ Order $\rightarrow$ Class $\rightarrow$ Phylum $\rightarrow$ Kingdom $\rightarrow$ Domain
9. **Geologic Time Scale (Oldest/Earliest to Youngest/Latest)**
   Hadean Eon $\rightarrow$ Archean Eon $\rightarrow$ Proterozoic Eon $\rightarrow$ Paleozoic Era $\rightarrow$ Mesozoic Era $\rightarrow$ Cenozoic Era
10. **Elements in Earth's Crust (by Abundance)**
    Sodium $\rightarrow$ Potassium $\rightarrow$ Calcium $\rightarrow$ Magnesium $\rightarrow$ Iron $\rightarrow$ Aluminum $\rightarrow$ Silicon $\rightarrow$ Oxygen
11. **Mohs Hardness Scale (Softest to Hardest)**
    Talc (1) $\rightarrow$ Gypsum/Halite (2) $\rightarrow$ Calcite (3) $\rightarrow$ Fluorite (4) $\rightarrow$ Apatite (5) $\rightarrow$ Feldspar (6) $\rightarrow$ Quartz (7) $\rightarrow$ Topaz (8) $\rightarrow$ Corundum (9) $\rightarrow$ Diamond (10)
12. **Silicate Mineral Structures (Least Linked to Most Linked)**
    Isolated tetrahedra (Olivine) $\rightarrow$ Chains (Pyroxene) $\rightarrow$ Sheets (Mica) $\rightarrow$ 3D Frameworks (Feldspar, Quartz)
13. **Igneous Magmas (by Silica Content / Viscosity, Lowest to Highest)**
    Ultramafic $\rightarrow$ Mafic $\rightarrow$ Intermediate $\rightarrow$ Felsic
